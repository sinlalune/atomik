# Auditor

The auditor reads the exact candidate diff, governing
[architecture](./architecture.md), relevant decisions and module notes, and
overlapping live paths.

The auditor may be a person or a capable agent. Its findings and verdict are
recorded in the candidate-bound audit. Automation verifies the record's
identity and completeness, not the quality of its reasoning.
