S08s landed `ADR-022` and updated the documents it was looking at. It did not
sweep, and the result was a repository that contradicted itself at the first
thing a participant reads: the gate printed `path history forbidden (no
rewriting; retention off)` while `paths.md` — required reading item 1 — still
listed "publish an append-only retention ref" as step 8 of every work unit and
told the reader to "rebase on the current trunk".

This is the S08q finding a second time, and the repetition is the point. Fixing
the pages in front of me was not enough on either occasion, so this unit ends
with a predicate rather than another careful pass.

## What moved

- **`paths.md`** — work-unit step 8 is conditional; `## Retain every completed
  checkpoint` becomes `## Keep every completed checkpoint reachable`, split into
  the `forbidden` default and the `retained` alternative; producing `C` merges
  the trunk in rather than rebasing onto it.
- **`execution-protocol.md`** — retention is required only where the host
  retains; the closure list's four rewriting steps collapse into one
  policy-aware step and the list is renumbered; the provisional-commit paragraph
  stops instructing an unconditional fold.
- **`repair.md`**, **`repository-layout.md`** — retention procedures and the ref
  namespace are marked as belonging to a retaining host, with the no-rewrite
  equivalent named.
- **`tools/cairn-audit.mjs`** and its fixture — the generated audit template
  said "Run after the rebase" and "the rebased diff"; it now names the candidate
  and the trunk it will land on.
- **`.github/workflows/cairn.yml`** — the retention fetch STAYS, with a comment
  saying why: it is harmless when the namespace is empty, it keeps this file
  correct as the adapter a `retained` host copies, and deleting it is how a host
  that switches back gets a green run over an unfetched namespace — S08b's exact
  failure, reintroduced as a cleanup.
- **`ADR-012`** — annotated as amended by `ADR-022`: the rebase gate keeps its
  requirement and loses its operation.

## A false claim about the host, corrected

`plan.md` item 10 asserted "the trunk now requires a pull request". It does not.
`gh api repos/.../branches/master/protection` returns `Branch not protected`,
which is consistent with the declared `ci` tier and with
`transport.integration: manual-git`. The claim is corrected in place rather than
deleted, because the useful fact is that a document asserted something about its
own host that the host contradicts.

That also settles the open transport question: integration remains a direct push
of the locally built, locally tested merge commit, so checked identity and landed
identity are equal by construction. No pull-request adapter is needed, and none
is invented.
