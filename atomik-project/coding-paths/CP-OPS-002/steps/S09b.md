## What was found, in one paragraph

The protocol has two weights. The entry chain is the light, portable,
harness-agnostic thing the manifesto describes. The body behind it is not: the
weight went into closure mechanics, one and a half of the manifesto's six
stages, while the upstream stages the owner lives in — ideation, research,
specification, roadmap — are host doctrine in Atomik and portable nowhere. The
concept knowledge base, which the manifesto calls the core, is finished for the
protocol's own vocabulary and unshaped for a project's or for coding. Against
the state of the art, Cairn is ahead on durable execution state and honest
closure, aligned on `AGENTS.md` and worktrees, and behind on packaging — Agent
Skills — which is also the manifesto's token principle made standard.
