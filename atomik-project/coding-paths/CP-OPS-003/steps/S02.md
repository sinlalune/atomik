# CP-OPS-003 S02

### S02 — one specification — **COMPLETE**

#### Plan

Move the concept wiki out of the 0.2 specification copy to `docs/concepts/`, a
root this repository owns; point `roots.concepts` at it; stop every live page
presenting the copy as current; and retire the copy rather than deleting it,
under the scope amendment this record carries.

Advances outcome 3, as amended.

#### Work

```cairn-unit
step: S02
unit: 02

verified: cairn-check, CI on the branch
```

- `git mv docs/cairn/specification/concepts docs/concepts` — 73 flat pages and
  the three folders the 1.1 adoption wrote, bodies byte-identical.
- `roots.concepts` → `docs/concepts` in `cairn.config.json`.
- 182 broken relative links in 10 files, repaired by where the file stands, not
  by rule of thumb: seven are editable documentation — three ADRs, `paths.md`,
  `docs/index.md`, `docs/cairn/index.md`, and the retired specification's own
  index — and were repointed at the wiki's new root. Three are append-only
  records under `CP-OPS-002/steps/`, and they link four concept pages by exact
  path. Those four keep a forwarding stub where they stood. The stub is the
  amendment's whole cost, and it is what a repository that forbids rewriting
  its history has instead of a repair.
- `docs/cairn/specification/index.md` carries a RETIRED banner naming the
  release that supersedes it and saying why the tree is kept.
- the live routes into it are gone: `AGENTS.md` step 3, `binding.md`'s banner
  and its concept-wiki row, `docs/index.md`, `docs/cairn/index.md`,
  `docs/bedrock/index.md`, `docs/bedrock/22_22-agent-handoff.md` and
  `docs/agents/index.md` now name `cairn/README.md`, the installed release.
  `paths.md` is a 0.2 page whose own retirement is S03's; it carries a banner
  naming the copy retired and is not silently left.
- the live wiki stopped naming the retired copy as its normative parent
  (`docs/concepts/index.md`), and the four concept pages that cite a 0.2
  section now say *retired 0.2* where they cite it.
- `cairn.lock.json` follows the move: the three kit-written concept indexes are
  repathed, digests untouched and still matching, so `status` reads them
  pristine rather than missing. See the self-review for what was deliberately
  NOT synced.
- `tools/cairn-{rules,spec-build,init}.mjs` deleted with their `package.json`
  scripts and the `cairn-spec:build` line in `binding.md`'s contract. This is
  S04's work pulled forward, and it is pulled forward because this unit broke
  it: `cairn-spec-build.mjs` resolves the concept root from a hard-coded
  `docs/cairn/specification`, so the move made `npm run cairn-spec:build` crash
  with `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'title')`. The choices
  were to repair a tool the path had already decided to delete, or to delete it
  now. `docs/cairn/specification.html` is therefore frozen at the 0.2 cut and
  `docs/cairn/index.md` says so where it offers it.
- the `concept-growth` advisory reports 72 new articles. **None are new.** The
  rule reads `roots.concepts`, the root moved, and every page under it is
  therefore unseen to a rule comparing against the trunk. They earned their
  pages in CP-OPS-002; this unit moved them and wrote none.

#### Self-review

- `delete:` `tools/cairn-{rules,spec-build,init}.mjs` and two scripts, rather
  than repairing a path constant in a tool the path deletes two units later.
- `shrink:` the four stubs from six lines of explanation each to two body lines.
- `shrink:` the scope amendment's closing paragraph, which defended the ruling
  rather than recording it.
- `refused:` syncing `cairn.config.json`'s digest in `cairn.lock.json`. The
  manifest records what the kit wrote; a file this repository edited must read
  as edited, or the lock stops being evidence and becomes decoration. The three
  concept paths were repathed because a move is not an edit — the kit derives
  those paths from `roots.concepts`, the digests still match byte for byte, and
  the alternative was `status` reporting three kit files missing. Measured
  after: 0 missing, 11 edited, every one of the 11 genuinely edited.
- net: −1 372 lines, +4 stub files.
