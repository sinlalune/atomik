#### The S02 deferral, reversed with its reason

S02 deferred to this unit the deletion of the retired tree's non-load-bearing
files — `specification/index.md` and the reference pages — on the ground that
only six files are load-bearing for frozen records. **Refused, and this is the
reversal.**

Measured rather than estimated, because S02's own figures were not:
`reference/` holds **nine** pages beside its index, two of which frozen records
name. `specification/index.md` is **1 893** lines and makes **17** links into
`reference/`, across **10** distinct targets. Deleting seven pages means
editing that index, and editing two live concept pages —
`docs/concepts/handoff.md:40` and `docs/concepts/project-memory.md:38` — which
cite `handoff-brief.md` and `repository-layout.md`. The
result is half a document: a retained specification index whose reference
chapters are gone. Rewriting a retired document to survive its own amputation
is the thing this path has refused at `paths.md`, at the journal, at the round
briefs and at bedrock's dated records. Either the tree is history and stays
whole, or it goes — and it cannot go, because six of its pages are named by
records that may not be edited.
