# Coherence audit — CP-OPS-002 @ e409e85

Run on the exact candidate, before acceptance. The judgement in this record is not a
machine verdict; its existence, completeness, and exact candidate binding are
mechanical closure gates. Its job is to catch what deterministic checks cannot
— two paths that each pass every rule and still pull the architecture in
different directions.

## What to read

- the candidate diff for this branch, against the trunk it will land on
- every architecture page and decision record named in this path's documentation coverage
- the module area notes the diff touches
- any OTHER path currently `running` that declares an overlapping surface

## Findings

### Does the diff contradict an accepted decision?

No. The candidate adds seven decision records (ADR-016 to ADR-022) and every
one is `accepted` by the owner with a dated ruling. The two that reverse an
earlier decision say so in their own header: ADR-020 extends ADR-009, ADR-012
and ADR-019; ADR-022 supersedes ADR-021 as the default path-history policy and
amends ADR-019 decision 1 and the ADR-012 rebase gate, with ADR-021 retained as
the design for a `retained` host. The remaining fifteen ADR changes are
frontmatter backfill (S05) with no change of decision. Bedrock 22 became a
pointer to the portable route and bedrock 24 gained the ceremony schema; both
were owner-ruled at the opening check (rulings 5 and 9) and the pre-extraction
body of bedrock 22 is retained verbatim under `docs/bedrock/archive/`.

### Does it duplicate something another running path is building?

No. The other running paths are `CP-MVP-011` and `CP-MVP-012`, both
grandfathered and both living only on their own branches: their records are
not on the trunk, so their `writes:` could not be read from here, and the live
view describes them as product work (Wikimedia augmentation and link graph).
This candidate changes no file under `apps/`, `packages/` or `shared/` — zero
product files against the trunk — and its declared surface is protocol tooling
and documentation. The one shared file both kinds of path touch is the
generated `ACTIVE.md`, which is regenerated rather than edited and which the
merge of either grandfathered path will regenerate again.

### Did it introduce architecture that belongs in an ADR and has none?

Two things were introduced without a decision record, and both are stated as
proposals rather than decisions. First, `cairn.config.json` schema 1 as the
executable host binding (S08p) rests on ADR-020's artefact boundary and on the
configuration reference page, which carries its status; it did not get its own
ADR. Second, the Cairn 1.0 shape in the manifesto convergence record — six
chapters, pull-request transport, skills, `npx cairn` — is owner-ruled in a
session note but is deliberately not an ADR here, because ruling 11 sends it to
the genesis repository, where it will be that repository's first decision.
Neither is architecture of the product; both are architecture of the protocol,
and the specification's own conformance page names the first as implemented
and the second as not yet.

### Is anything now documented in two places that will drift apart?

Yes, three pairs, each with a stated resolution and one still open.

- **The portable route and the host pointer.** `paths.md`, the execution
  protocol and `binding.md` are the required reading; bedrock 22 now points to
  them and `paths-history.md` and the archived bedrock 22 body are linked
  history. The old and new say different things by design, and both old copies
  say they are not current rules.
- **The rule catalogue and the conformance linkage** in the specification are
  generated from the checker (`cairn-rules.mjs`) and a test fails the build if
  the shipped table drifts. Not a drift risk.
- **The portable corpus inside Atomik and the future `cairn` repository.**
  This is the open one. Until the genesis commit, `docs/cairn/specification/`
  is the only authority; forward-plan item 12 and the owner's ruling 10 make the
  genesis commit the single cutover, after which Atomik holds installed copies
  under a lock file. The risk is real and bounded to the interval before that
  commit, in which no second copy exists.

Noted and not blocking: the owner's two root notes (`cairn-manifesto`,
`cairn-project`) exist untracked beside their verbatim copies under
`docs/cairn/`; the copies say where they came from.

## Verdict

**Drift noted, proceeding.** No accepted decision is contradicted, no running
path is duplicated, and the two undecided introductions are named as proposals
in their own text. The one drift that matters — a second copy of the portable
corpus — cannot exist before the genesis commit and is resolved by it.
