# Journal

One file per entry: `YYYY-MM-DD-<path-id>.md`. Two paths writing two files
never conflict, which is what lets every path merge itself without a
gatekeeper (`../coding-paths/paths.md`). Chronology comes from the filenames.

`../log.md` is the FROZEN archive — every entry up to 2026-08-14, when the
journal moved here. Its ~300 entries are deliberately not migrated: rewriting
recorded history to fit a newer convention would be worse than the convention.
Read it for anything before that date.

An entry records INTEGRATED work: what changed, why, what it cost, and what was
deviated from. Written at merge time by the path that did the work.
