# CP-MVP-010 — retrieval over the graph and vault-grounded chat

Closed the back half of M8 with one file-first retrieval pipeline:

1. A dependency-free BM25F core now ranks path, filename, title, headings,
   frontmatter, link text, and body with phrase positions, diacritic folding,
   inspectable field contributions, and the `titles · linked · full` reach
   ladder. It replaces the substring engine behind the existing search
   perimeters instead of adding a second search stack.
2. The lazy `.atomik/index/` projection rebuilds byte-identically from files,
   patches individual saves where safe, and shares one maintenance door and
   push signal with the graph index. Graph expansion is typed, budgeted,
   hub-aware, and pure.
3. Bounded ContextPackets show every selected entry's stage/reason and every
   omission's reason, plus the `covered · thin · empty` verdict. Retrieval
   ActionTrace lines record stages, counts, estimated tokens, latency, and zero
   external billing without recording queries or content.
4. Chat can retrieve before generation, inspect the exact per-turn packet, and
   reopen its persisted packet/citation maps. Numbered markers and phrase links
   open the vault notes they cite; unresolved numbers remain visible. The
   final owner bench pinned the centred conversation lane, decimal-safe
   sentence extents, and complete multi-sentence blockquote extents.
5. Ranked search explains why each result matched and exposes vault-wide broken
   links. The generated D15 workflow and dated evaluation record make future
   storage/semantic stages compare against evidence rather than preference.

The evaluation finishes at 100% recall@5 (13/13), MRR 0.955. The real 115-file
corpus built in 154–167 ms, queried at p95 0.4 ms, and serialized to 8.4 MiB;
ADR-013 records the 100 MB/latency/build/corpus triggers that can reopen the
storage choice.
