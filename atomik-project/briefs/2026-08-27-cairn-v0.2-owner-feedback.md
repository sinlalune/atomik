# Owner feedback — Cairn v0.2

Delivered 2026-08-27, partly as a root-level file and partly in session. Retained
here because the corrections it contains are doctrine-level, not editorial: two of
them say the specification asserts the opposite of what it implements. Answered in
[S07o](../coding-paths/CP-OPS-002/index.md).

## Editorial

- `atomik-project/log.md` "frozen compatibility archive" — a remnant of the old
  protocol, from before parallel paths; `log/` is now the only journal.
- `atomik-project` — "No it is `project`. atomik is the one we build upon but it is
  not universal, rename every mention."
- **route link** — leads to the lightweight-path concept page.
- **blob object id** — no explanation; `git rev-parse HEAD:docs/architecture/example.md`
  is dropped without any, and the reader may not know such specific git commands.
- `/sources` and `/projects` are remnants of the repository's Atomik vault
  configuration — "I used the repo as the first vault test."
- `refs/cairn/checkpoints/<path-id>/<n>` is talked about numerous times but never
  localised on the repo.

## Design

Global: the aesthetic is "quite classic" for the intended refinement. "Refined
research aesthetic doesn't mean old, it means clear and modern (research)" — so a
more modern font, and some rounded corners to flatten the tension a little.

Specific:

- active tab / active tree-panel page — no top or side line highlight; a simple
  subtle background change. As it stands it adds a weird layer over the desired
  minimal separation lines.
- get rid of the Pane A and Pane B labels.
- the active-system-tab load doesn't work: keep the Cairn full spec on the left and
  open the rest on the right side.
