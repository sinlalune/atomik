## The rulings

All twenty-four checklist items are accepted as written. Grouped as the record
groups them:

1. **Weight.** Cairn 1.0 is cut to a stated weight budget before it is
   published (specification under 8,000 words, entry chain under 3,000, kit
   under 30 files, one lightweight unit under 6 protocol files — targets,
   measured at release).
2. **Engine.** Per-path events fold into the path folder; `index.md` stays per
   folder and `log.md` retires in path folders and the specification; one
   journal; one portable root in the kit.
3. **Brain.** The three-scope concept wiki (protocol, project, coding) is a 1.0
   deliverable ahead of any further checker work. *Decide item answered:*
   learning notes are a **Cairn** artefact, as the manifesto's pedagogy section
   argues.
4. **Chronology.** The six stages are the spine of the 1.0 specification.
   *Decide item answered by accepting the record's split:* the brainstorm note,
   the decision record and the roadmap register are portable from Atomik as
   they are; bedrock's product pages are Atomik-only.
5. **Approach.** The pull request is the default integration transport, with
   `cairn-check` as the one required status check and `manual-git` as the
   no-forge fallback; closing acceptance is the review approval plus the
   journal entry the merge writes.
6. **Code.** A coding stance ships as a skill, not a checker rule; a work unit
   is plan → change → self-review → verify, with plan and review as sections of
   the step record.
7. **Engagement and pedagogy.** The kit installs the thin surface and links the
   specification at the pinned release; 1.0 pages follow the writing rule (one
   idea per page, plain definition first, examples before rules).
8. **Project.** One path, one folder; nothing else per path except the journal
   entry at integration.
9. **State of the art.** Procedures ship as Agent Skills; `depends_on:` joins
   the path record and "unblocked" the live view, with no graph database;
   compatibility with spec-driven toolkits is stated rather than contested.
10. **Final form.** The six-chapter spine, the keep and retire lists and the
    weight budget are the opening acceptance of the genesis path, with the
    owner's right to strike any line; `npx cairn` is the distribution
    mechanism; the genesis commit is the authority cutover and Atomik is the
    first adopter.
11. **Sequence.** CP-OPS-002 closes without further protocol work; the genesis
    opens in the new repository with the convergence record and the manifesto
    as its opening input; the first queued project is adopter number one and
    its friction sets the 1.1 agenda.
